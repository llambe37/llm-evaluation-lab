# Evaluation 01: Factual Accuracy and Hallucination Detection

## User Prompt

What is the capital of Australia, and why was it chosen as the capital?

## AI Response

Sydney is the capital of Australia. It was selected because it was already the country's largest and most economically important city when Australia became a federation in 1901.

## Evaluation

**Rating: Incorrect**

The response contains a major factual error. Sydney is not the capital of Australia; Canberra is.

The response also builds additional reasoning on top of the incorrect premise by claiming that Sydney was selected as the capital. This demonstrates how one factual error can lead to additional unsupported statements.

### Issues Identified

- Incorrectly identifies Sydney as Australia's capital.
- Provides a fabricated explanation based on that incorrect claim.
- Presents incorrect information confidently without indicating uncertainty.
- Fails the primary requirement of factual accuracy.

## Improved Response

Canberra is the capital of Australia. It was selected as a compromise between Sydney and Melbourne, which were competing to become the national capital after Australian federation. The site for Canberra was chosen in New South Wales, and the city was purpose-built to serve as the country's capital.

## Evaluation Skills Demonstrated

- Factual accuracy assessment
- Hallucination detection
- Identification of unsupported reasoning
- Response correction
- Clear written justification
