# Evaluation 03: Reasoning and Consistency

## User Prompt

A store offers 20% off a $50 item. After the discount, a 10% sales tax is applied. What is the final price?

## AI Response

The 20% discount on $50 is $10, so the discounted price is $40.

A 10% sales tax on the original $50 price is $5.

Therefore, the final price is $45.

## Evaluation

**Rating:** Incorrect

The response correctly calculates the discounted price as $40 but then applies the sales tax to the wrong amount. The prompt states that the 10% tax is applied after the discount, so the tax must be calculated from $40, not the original $50 price.

This is a reasoning error because the individual calculations are mathematically valid, but the sequence of operations does not follow the conditions in the prompt.

## Improved Response

The 20% discount on $50 is $10, making the discounted price $40.

The 10% sales tax on $40 is $4.

Therefore, the final price is **$44**.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Mathematical reasoning
- Logical consistency
- Instruction interpretation
- Accuracy
- Clear explanation
