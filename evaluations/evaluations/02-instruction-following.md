# Evaluation 02: Instruction-Following

## User Prompt

Explain photosynthesis in exactly three bullet points. Each bullet must contain no more than 15 words.

## AI Response

- Photosynthesis is the process plants use to convert sunlight into chemical energy stored as glucose.
- Plants absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere and water from the soil through their roots.
- Oxygen is produced as a byproduct and released into the atmosphere, supporting life on Earth.

## Evaluation

**Rating:** Needs improvement

The response is factually accurate and uses exactly three bullet points. However, it does not fully follow the user's explicit length constraint. The second and third bullets exceed the maximum of 15 words.

This demonstrates an important distinction in LLM evaluation: a response can be factually correct while still failing instruction-following requirements.

## Improved Response

- Plants use sunlight to convert water and carbon dioxide into glucose.
- Chlorophyll captures light energy that powers the chemical reactions.
- Photosynthesis produces oxygen, which plants release into the atmosphere.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Instruction adherence
- Constraint compliance
- Factual accuracy
- Relevance
- Clarity
